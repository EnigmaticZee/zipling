Open-source components

Zipling's Arca engine is built on these open-source projects, used under their licences:

  • Lepton JPEG (Microsoft's Rust port of Dropbox's Lepton), Apache-2.0
  • preflate-rs (Microsoft), Apache-2.0
  • Zstandard (Meta), BSD-3-Clause, via the zstd crate (MIT)
  • XZ Utils liblzma, 0BSD, via the liblzma crate (MIT or Apache-2.0)
  • BLAKE3, CC0-1.0 or Apache-2.0
  • FastCDC, MIT
  • PPMd (ppmd-rust), CC0-1.0 or MIT-0
  • Rayon, MIT or Apache-2.0

To open other archive formats, Zipling runs 7-Zip by Igor Pavlov, unmodified, as a separate helper program. 7-Zip is licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later, with some files under BSD licences and its RAR decoder under the unRAR licence restriction. Its exact source is available from the 7-Zip project.

For classic Mac formats and file names in legacy encodings, Zipling runs lsar and unar from The Unarchiver's XADMaster by Dag Ågren and MacPaw, unmodified, as separate helper programs. They are licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later. Their exact source is available from XADMaster and UniversalDetector.
