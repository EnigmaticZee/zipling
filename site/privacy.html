Privacy

Your files are yours.

Zipling is built to collect nothing: not your files, not your usage, not who you are.

Last updated 26 October 2026

This website

  • No cookies, analytics, trackers or advertising.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Everything loads from this site.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The Zipling app

  • Zipling works entirely on your Mac. Your files and archives are never uploaded anywhere.
  • There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry.
  • So Spotlight can find files inside your archives, Zipling adds the names of the files in archives you open to your Mac's own Spotlight index. That index never leaves your Mac, and you can turn this off and remove everything Zipling added in Settings.
  • If a future version adds anything that connects to the internet, such as checking for updates, this page will say exactly what is sent before that version is released.

Bug reports

Bug reports are filed on GitHub and are public. Please don't attach personal files or include personal information. GitHub's terms and privacy statement apply to anything you post there.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on GitHub.
