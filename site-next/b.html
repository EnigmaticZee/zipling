Good to know.

Can people without Zipling open my archives?

Yes. Zipling's own format gives you the smallest archive. When you share with someone who doesn't have Zipling, choose ZIP or 7z instead and it opens anywhere, with a password and split parts if you like.

Is “smaller” really free? Do I lose any quality?

Nothing is lost. A photo comes back as the exact same file, bit for bit, not a re-saved copy. Zipling proves it to itself: it rebuilds each original while compressing, before keeping the smaller form, and checks every part again when extracting.

What if an archive gets damaged?

If you added repair data when you made it, Zipling rebuilds the damaged parts exactly, even when the end of the file is missing. If the damage is beyond what the repair data covers, it tells you which files are lost instead of guessing.

Can it shrink the zips I already have?

Yes. Convert turns any archive Zipling opens into a Zipling archive, or into a tighter ZIP or 7z. It shows the new size before you start and checks the result before it offers to trash the old one.

When can I download it, and what will it cost?

Not yet. Zipling is in active development. It will cost $19.99, paid once: no subscription, and updates are included.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon.
