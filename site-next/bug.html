Help

Report a bug.

Something went wrong? Tell us by email. It is private by default, needs no account, and a person reads every message.

Email us

One message with the five things below is usually all we need. Screenshots help; personal files do not, so please leave them out unless we ask.

Email bugs@zipling.app →

The button opens a message with the questions already in it.

What to include

  1. What you did. The steps, in order, as plainly as you can.
  2. What happened, and what you expected instead.
  3. Your macOS version and Zipling's, from Zipling › About Zipling.
  4. The archive's format and size if one is involved. Do not send the archive itself unless we ask.
  5. A screenshot, if there is something to see.

What happens next

You get a reply within a few days, a question or two if we need them, and the fix ships in the next update. If an archive turned out damaged, say so first: those messages go to the front of the queue.

Privacy

Your message stays between you and us. Nothing in it is published, and Zipling itself never sends anything from your Mac.
